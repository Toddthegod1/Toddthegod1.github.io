**Stack:** Python, web scraping, NLP  
**Award:** Winner, Dickinson College Innovation Competition

## Overview
Benny is an AI-powered chatbot that answers Dickinson-specific student questions. I co-built it with a student team through Devil Labs, and it won Dickinson's Innovation Competition.

## Problem
General-purpose chatbots don't know the details of a specific campus, so they give vague or wrong answers about things like offices, services, and policies. Students need answers grounded in their own college's information.

## What I Built
- Scraping pipelines that collect and structure Dickinson-specific data
- An NLP-powered chatbot that draws on that structured data to give accurate, campus-specific answers

## Outcome
Benny won Dickinson's Innovation Competition. The project taught me how much of a chatbot's usefulness comes from the data behind it: clean, well-structured source data mattered as much as the model.
